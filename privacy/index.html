Privacy

Lewos collects personal data only when you send it to us yourself.

What we collect

When you join First Access or book, we collect your name, email address, chosen weekend, group size, the confirmations made during booking and anything you choose to tell us. When you use the question form, we collect your name, email address and question. To protect the forms from automated misuse, we temporarily process a one-way technical identifier derived from your network address and email address. It cannot be used by us to recover either value.

Why

To manage First Access, reserve and administer your booking, take payment, arrange your stay, comply with our legal obligations, or answer a question you send us.

Where it goes

Questions sent through the contact form are delivered through Netlify, the company that hosts this website. First Access requests, seat allocations and booking records are processed and stored using Supabase. Payments are handled by Stripe; Lewos does not receive or store your full card details. Transactional email may be delivered through Resend. These providers process the information only to provide those services to Lewos. We do not sell your details, share them with advertisers or add you to a list you did not request.

How long we keep it

Booking and contact details are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until it is clear you are not joining — whichever comes first. The technical identifier stops affecting access after its fifteen-minute protection window and is deleted automatically during subsequent security cleanup.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. One email is enough and we will act on it.
