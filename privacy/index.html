Privacy

Lewos collects personal data only when you send it to us yourself.

What we collect

When you claim a seat at The Lewos Tavern, we collect your name, your email address, the weekend you're interested in, the size of your group, and anything you choose to tell us.

Why

To let you know when booking opens, and to arrange your stay if you decide to join.

Where it goes

Your request is delivered to us by Netlify, the company that hosts this website, and forwarded to our email. We do not sell your details, we do not share them with advertisers, and we do not add you to any list you did not ask for.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until it is clear you are not joining — whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. One email is enough and we will act on it.
