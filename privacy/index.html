Privacy

Lewos collects personal data only when you send it to us yourself.

What we collect

When you join First Access or book, we collect your name, email address, chosen weekend, group size, payment and booking status, the confirmations made during booking and anything you choose to tell us. Stripe handles your card details; Lewos does not receive or store the complete card number. When you use the question form, we collect your name, email address and question. To protect the forms from automated misuse, we temporarily process a one-way technical identifier derived from your network address and email address. It cannot be used by us to recover either value.

If you attend a filmed Tavern weekend, we may also process your filming preference and, where you consent, recordings containing your identifiable image or voice. The purpose and choices are explained separately before booking and filming.

Why

To manage First Access, reserve and administer your booking, take payment, arrange your stay, comply with our legal obligations, or answer a question you send us.

Where it goes

Questions sent through the contact form are delivered through Netlify, the company that hosts this website. First Access requests, seat allocations and booking records are processed and stored using Supabase. Payments are handled by Stripe; Lewos does not receive or store your full card details. Transactional email may be delivered through Resend. These providers process the information only to provide those services to Lewos. We do not sell your details, share them with advertisers or add you to a list you did not request.

How long we keep it

Unsuccessful First Access requests and ordinary contact questions are kept only as long as needed to manage the request and reasonable follow-up. Confirmed booking, payment and invoice records are kept for the periods required by applicable accounting, tax and consumer law. A deletion request cannot override a legal duty to retain a particular record.

Filming preferences and consent records are kept for as long as needed to demonstrate and respect your choice. Recordings approved for use are kept while that use remains relevant, unless consent is withdrawn for future use. The technical identifier stops affecting access after its fifteen-minute protection window and is deleted automatically during subsequent security cleanup.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, restrict or delete personal data we hold about you, object where processing relies on a legitimate interest, or withdraw consent for future use where consent is the basis. These rights can be subject to legal exceptions. One email is enough to start the request.
