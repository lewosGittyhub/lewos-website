The booking

A featured Tavern weekend has six seats. A group is kept together: if the complete group no longer fits, payment does not begin and no partial group is booked.

Temporary seat hold

When checkout begins, the complete group is held for up to 40 minutes while payment is completed. Starting checkout alone does not confirm a booking. Confirmation follows successful payment.

First Access invitation

A First Access invitation is personal to the invited booking and remains available for the period stated in the invitation. Seats that are not paid for within that period may be released.

Minimum group

A featured weekend runs with at least four paid guests. If Lewos cancels because that minimum is not reached, the affected guest receives the amount paid back in full.

Age and filming

Featured Tavern weekends are for adults aged 18 or older. Filming information and each adult guest's separate choice about identifiable promotional use are explained in the legal notice.
