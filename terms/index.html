Draft booking terms.

This page is being prepared before paid public booking opens. It is not yet a final offer or set of booking conditions.

Payment remains closed while this document is completed. The legal identity, tourism-registration and insolvency-protection details must still be inserted and checked before these terms can enter into force.

Organiser

The organiser is Robert Neugebauer, operating as Lewos in Asturias, Spain. Email: lewos.co@gmail.com.

To complete before sales: full business address, tax identification, applicable tourism-registration number, telephone contact and the name and full contact details of the insolvency-protection provider.

The booking

A featured Tavern weekend has six seats and is intended for adults aged 18 or older. The services, dates, accommodation, meals, transfers, programme, total price including applicable taxes, payment method and any accessibility or special requirements accepted by Lewos form part of the booking confirmation and the pre-contract travel information.

A group is kept together. If the complete group no longer fits, payment does not begin and no partial group is booked.

Temporary seat hold and confirmation

When checkout begins, the complete group is held for up to 40 minutes while payment is completed. Starting checkout alone does not confirm a booking. A booking becomes binding when payment is successfully accepted. Lewos then provides the booking confirmation and accepted terms on a durable medium, normally by email. A failure or delay in delivering that email does not undo a successfully paid booking.

First Access invitation

A First Access invitation is personal to the invited booking and remains available for the period stated in the invitation. Seats that are not paid for within that period may be released. The price and services shown in the invitation and checkout apply to the resulting booking.

Transfer to another traveller

The traveller may transfer the booking to another person who meets all applicable conditions by notifying Lewos on a durable medium at least seven calendar days before the weekend begins. The original and replacement traveller are jointly responsible for any outstanding balance and for reasonable, evidenced costs actually caused by the transfer. Lewos will provide evidence of those costs on request.

Cancellation by the traveller

The traveller may cancel before the weekend begins. Any cancellation charge will be appropriate and justifiable and will not exceed the booking price minus costs saved and income received from reselling the released services. Lewos will explain the calculation on request.

No cancellation charge applies where unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at or near the destination significantly affect the weekend or travel to it, as provided by applicable package-travel law. In that situation, payments are refunded in full without additional compensation.

Cancellation by Lewos

A featured weekend requires at least four paid guests. If that minimum is not reached, Lewos may cancel by notifying the traveller no later than seven calendar days before the weekend begins. Lewos may also cancel where unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances prevent performance, provided notice is given without undue delay before the start.

In either case, all payments received for the cancelled booking are refunded in full. Any refund required under these cancellation provisions will be made without undue delay and no later than fourteen calendar days after termination.

Price and changes before the weekend

The total price shown in the paid booking confirmation is fixed and will not be increased afterwards.

Lewos will communicate any other proposed change clearly on a durable medium. If a main characteristic must change significantly or an agreed special requirement cannot be met, the traveller may accept the change or terminate without a cancellation charge within the reasonable response period stated in the notice.

During the weekend

Lewos is responsible for performing the included services in accordance with the booking, including services supplied by third parties. A guest should report any problem without undue delay so that it can be remedied. Where a significant service cannot be supplied, Lewos will provide a suitable alternative without additional cost where reasonably possible and apply any price reduction or other remedy required by law.

Complaints and assistance

Questions, requests for assistance and complaints can be sent to lewos.co@gmail.com. Lewos will acknowledge and handle them directly. The booking confirmation will include a telephone contact for urgent assistance during the weekend and the applicable consumer-dispute information.

Age, filming and personal data

Featured Tavern weekends are for adults aged 18 or older. Filming information and each adult guest's separate choice about identifiable promotional use are explained in the legal notice. Personal data is handled as described in the privacy information.

Applicable rights

These terms do not limit mandatory consumer or package-travel rights. Spanish law applies, without depriving a consumer of mandatory protections that apply in their country of habitual residence. The competent courts are determined by applicable consumer law.
