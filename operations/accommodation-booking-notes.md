# Accommodation booking — agreed direction

## Confirmed rules

- A Tavern booking always includes the fixed Tavern weekend (Friday–Monday).
- Optional extended stay runs from Monday 15:00 before the weekend until Friday 10:00 after it.
- The accommodation capacity is capped at 12 guests at the same time.
- Cabana Dobra: 2 double bedrooms, up to 4 guests.
- Cabana Bulnes: 1 double bedroom, up to 2 guests.
- Cabana Covadonga: 1 double bedroom, up to 2 guests.
- Cabana Astur: 2 double bedrooms, up to 4 guests.
- Guests should choose how many double bedrooms they need; couples may share a room.
- An extra night in the regular accommodation costs 100 EUR per apartment/night, without meals.
- If the regular accommodation is full, an alternative house may be offered at 130 EUR per house/night.
- The alternative rate applies only to extra nights outside the fixed Friday–Monday Tavern weekend.
- If no suitable accommodation is available, show “Request availability”; do not take payment until confirmed.
