# Release candidate, 29 August 2026

Fifty commits ahead of `origin/main`. Fifty-seven files, roughly 4.000 lines added.
Prepared, tested and reviewed. **Not pushed and not deployed.** Both of those wait for
Robert's explicit word, and one of them is blocked anyway — see the last section.

## What a visitor gets that they do not have today

- **A new hero photograph**, Robert's own panorama of the valley, with a separate upright
  crop so a phone gets a picture instead of a sliver.
- **A weekend calendar** in the First Access form: pick a weekend by clicking a day rather
  than choosing from a menu, with the price for the party size beside the number of guests.
  *This appears only after the database migration below has run.* Until then the page shows
  the menu it shows today.
- **A slideshow of six photographs of Asturias**, one at a time, advancing every three
  seconds, steerable by arrow, dot, swipe or keyboard, and stopping the moment a visitor
  takes over.
- **A page for private Tavern requests** at `/tavern/private/`, reached from both the
  section button and the sign-up form.
- **Draft booking terms** at `/terms/` and **draft pre-contract travel information** at
  `/travel-information/`. Both carry `noindex` and say on their own face that they are not
  in force.
- **Shorter, more factual copy.** The Tavern page went from about 2.500 to about 2.300
  words with the repetition taken out.
- **Corrections a customer could otherwise have held against us**: the price now reads as a
  total including taxes with the main exclusion named, the promise that every bedroom sits
  ten metres away now mentions the house that is five hundred metres out, an unsupported
  claim about a first-access price is gone, and the heritage sentence names a source that
  can be checked.

## What is deliberately still closed

- **Paid booking is impossible.** `PUBLISHED_TERMS_VERSION`, `PUBLISHED_TERMS_DOCUMENT` and
  `PUBLISHED_TRAVEL_DOCUMENT` in `netlify/functions/_booking-config.mjs` are empty, and the
  checkout refuses while they are. Setting the environment switch alone changes nothing.
  Deploying this release does not bring the sale any closer to opening.
- The draft legal pages stay out of search and say so themselves.

## The one thing to do first, in this order

**Run `database/first-access.sql` in Supabase before deploying**, or accept that the
calendar stays hidden until you do. The migration adds `starts_on`, `ends_on` and
`price_cents` to `tavern_weekends`, plus `price_cents` on the seat claims. The site is built
to survive its absence: without those columns the calendar hides itself and the old menu
takes over, and the checkout — already closed — refuses rather than guesses a price. Nothing
breaks. The new feature simply does not appear.

Codex runs that migration together with `tests/database-integration.sql` in a transaction
that ends in `rollback` for the test, then for real.
