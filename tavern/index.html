Beyond the threshold

The outside world ends at the foot of the mountain.

There, the noise of ordinary life begins to fall away. Beyond it lie green valleys, mist, open sky and the quiet of Asturias. Somewhere ahead, a table is waiting for your party. You arrive carrying characters, questions and stories not yet written. Dinner is shared. Dice begin to roll. And somewhere between the first clue and the final breakfast, a collection of players becomes a party.

No clock counting down a four-hour session. No rush back into ordinary life. The tale is given room to breathe, to twist, to deepen and to draw you all the way in. For one long weekend, the mountains keep the outside world at a distance. There is only the table, the story and one question: what will your party do next?

Where the story begins

Welcome to Fontecha.

The Lewos Tavern is set within Complejo Rural de Fontecha, a small collection of mountain houses near Llerandi in Asturias.

The surrounding landscape has carried travellers for centuries. A Neolithic burial mound, the túmulo de Fontecha, is recorded on the boundary between Parres and Piloña, and old mountain paths still cross these ridges. Buried history and mountain folklore belong to this place. Now a new story is about to begin here.

Photographs of the real houses and the valley are published by the accommodation itself.

Meet your Game Master

This is Evan.

A storyteller with a love for horror, atmosphere and giving players a world they can truly lose themselves in.

Evan first joined a tabletop roleplaying game in a school library in Oxford almost ten years ago. When that group outgrew its table, he started a second one and stepped behind the screen. What began as a lunchtime game became eight years of storytelling, with some of that original group still at his table today.

His games combine detailed description, music and sound to make imagined places feel real. What matters most to him is not controlling the story, but watching players take ownership of it and continue talking about it after the session has ended.

Away from the table, Evan studies in London, works in esports production and brings experience from competitive sport and live-action roleplay. One day he hopes to run a game before a live audience.

We are genuinely delighted that he is coming to Asturias to lead the very first table at The Lewos Tavern.

Around the table

Questions people ask.

Has this run before?

No. Weekend 01 is the first one. Ever.

The room is built. The table is in it. The fire is lit. The campaign is written. Around sixteen hours of it, pulled out of Asturian myth that belongs to these mountains and nowhere else.

What hasn't happened yet is the first players walking through that door.

Everything here has been thought about: which chair you sit in, what comes to the table on Saturday night, what's waiting at the end of the story. A first edition is the one where none of it has settled yet. The people who show up still shape what this place turns into.

You'd be at the first table. There's only one of those.

How does First Access work?

You choose a weekend and we hold your seats. Nothing is paid today.

When booking opens, First Access guests get 24 hours to complete payment before the weekend goes public. We email you before that window starts, so you know when it begins.

If you do not book within those 24 hours, your seats are released and the weekend opens to everyone — first come, first served.

Your whole party is held together, or not at all. We do not split groups.

Do I need to have played before?

No. Tell us when you sign up and the Game Master builds a character with you and takes it from there. Bringing years of experience is fine too — the table is built around whoever ends up at it.

What language is it in?

English.

Where do I sleep?

In your own bedroom, roughly ten metres from the Tavern itself. One of the houses sits a little further out, about five hundred metres. You sleep where you play.

I'm coming alone. Is that strange?

Not at all. You may find a table of solo travellers, a few friends arriving together, or a mix of both. However the party comes together, arriving on your own should never make you feel like the odd one out.

That's what a tavern is for: sharing the same table, the same food, the same fire and one unfolding story. Robert hosts the weekend himself and will welcome you as he would a friend or family member, making sure everyone has a place in the party from the moment they arrive.

What's included?

Three nights, every meal, all non-alcoholic drinks, transfers between Asturias Airport or Arriondas train or bus station and the Tavern, the game, the professional Game Master, and the run of the Tavern. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are all taken care of throughout the weekend. Vegetarian, allergies or anything else can be arranged after booking. A welcome gift will be waiting on your seat when you sit down. Travel to Asturias is not included.

How do I get there?

Fly into Asturias (OVD), or come by train or bus to Arriondas. Travel to Asturias isn't included in the price, but your transfer from Asturias Airport or Arriondas station to the Tavern is.

Tell us how and when you plan to arrive, and we will arrange the last stretch. Collection from other locations and car hire can be arranged on request at additional cost. If you're driving, we'll send you the exact location and directions.

Will I be filmed?

Yes. Weekend 01 will be filmed by a professional videographer. Full details are set out in the filming and privacy terms provided before booking.

What if the weekend doesn't go ahead?

A weekend runs with a minimum of four players. If we don't reach that, it doesn't happen and you get every euro back. We will tell you no later than seven calendar days before the weekend begins.

Can I book the whole thing for my own group?

Yes. Private Tavern weekends run for 4 to 12 players, tailored to your group. Tell us what you have in mind through the form and we'll come back with a proposal.
