Not just a game night

Leave the real world at the tavern door.

This is a whole weekend shaped around the table: arriving as six players, sharing food, settling into the story and watching the party become something real. No rushing home. No packing the adventure into four hours. Just time, atmosphere and a campaign created to be remembered.

Everything around the table

Come ready to play. We take care of the rest.

  • Accommodation for the duration of your selected Tavern weekend
  • Meals and non-alcoholic drinks, from the arrival dinner through departure breakfast
  • A dedicated D&D experience and Dungeon Master
  • A place at your selected Tavern table—always limited to six guests

Flights are not included. Practical arrival and transfer details will be confirmed with guests before the weekend.

Make more of the journey

Want to stay a little longer?

The adventure does not have to begin on Friday or end on Monday. Depending on availability, extra nights can be requested from the Monday before your playing weekend through the Friday after it.

Rooms must be ready in time for the next party—but if you want to stay across two playing weekends, talk to us. That may be possible too.
