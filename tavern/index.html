Beyond the threshold

The outside world ends at the foot of the mountain.

There, the noise of ordinary life begins to fall away. Beyond it lie green valleys, mist, open sky and the quiet of Asturias. Somewhere ahead, a table is waiting for your party. You arrive carrying characters, questions and stories not yet written. Dinner is shared. Dice begin to roll. And somewhere between the first clue and the final breakfast, a collection of players becomes a party.

No clock counting down a four-hour session. No rush back into ordinary life. The tale is given room to breathe, to twist, to deepen and to draw you all the way in. For one long weekend, the mountains keep the outside world at a distance. There is only the table, the story and one question: what will your party do next?

The nearest airport is Asturias (OVD). Flights are not included, but tell us your arrival time and we'll help you sort the rest.

Around the table

Questions people ask.

Do I need to have played D&D before?

No. Half the table usually hasn't. The Dungeon Master builds the story around who shows up, and you'll be playing within ten minutes of sitting down.

What language is it in?

English.

Where do I sleep?

In your own bedroom, in an apartment a few minutes from the Tavern. Two bedrooms per apartment, so you'll share the place with one other player. Travelling as a couple? You can share a room instead.

I'm coming alone. Will I know anyone?

Nobody knows anyone. Six people meet on Friday evening and spend three days building one story together — that's the whole point. People arrive as strangers and leave as a party.

Who runs the game?

A professional Dungeon Master, hired for the weekend. Not a friend who plays a bit — someone who does this for a living.

What's included?

Three nights, every meal, all non-alcoholic drinks, the game, the Dungeon Master, your welcome kit and the run of the Tavern. Flights and anything you drink at the bar are not.

Will I be filmed?

At the opening weekend, yes — we're filming to show what this place is. Nothing is published without your written approval, and if you'd rather stay off camera we'll keep you out of every shot. Just say so when you book.

What if the weekend doesn't go ahead?

A weekend runs with a minimum of four players. If we don't reach that, the weekend doesn't happen and you get every euro back. You'll know well in advance.

Can I book the whole thing for my own group?

Yes. Private Tavern weekends run for 4 to 12 players, tailored to your group. Tell us what you have in mind through the form and we'll come back with a proposal.
