Price and payment

The displayed price is €2,025 per person including applicable taxes. Travel to Asturias, collection from other locations, optional car hire and personal expenditure are not included unless expressly added to the booking confirmation. Payment is made in one amount through Stripe. A group booking becomes binding after successful payment; Lewos then sends the written booking confirmation and accepted terms on a durable medium.

Minimum participation

If fewer than four guests have paid for a featured weekend, Lewos may cancel it by notifying guests no later than seven calendar days before it begins. Payments are then refunded in full within fourteen calendar days.

Travel documents and health

Guests are responsible for checking the passport, identity-document, visa and health requirements that apply to their nationality and circumstances when travelling to Spain. Any general information provided by Lewos does not replace advice from the relevant authority.

Accessibility and individual needs

Guests should contact Lewos before booking if reduced mobility, a disability, allergies, dietary needs or another individual requirement may affect participation. Lewos will provide available suitability information and confirm in writing any special requirement it accepts.

Cancellation, transfer and insurance

The guest may cancel before the weekend begins subject to an appropriate and justifiable charge based on actual losses, savings and resale. A booking may be transferred to another eligible traveller with at least seven calendar days' notice, subject only to evidenced transfer costs. Full details appear in the booking terms. Guests may choose to obtain personal travel and cancellation insurance; it is not included.

Key package-travel rights

Where the booking constitutes a package under Spanish package-travel law, Lewos is responsible for proper performance of the included services and for providing assistance where required. The traveller has statutory rights concerning significant changes, unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances, cancellation, refunds, remedies and insolvency protection. The final booking material will include the applicable statutory standard-information form and the guarantee provider's contact details.

Los detalles completos figuran en las condiciones de reserva. Los viajeros pueden contratar un seguro personal de viaje y cancelación; no está incluido.
